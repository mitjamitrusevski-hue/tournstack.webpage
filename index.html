We’re building TournStack to make sports tournaments easier to set up and run. Bring registrations, schedules, results and player updates together, with a public tournament page and live score displays that people can follow in their browser.

THE TOURNSTACK APPROACH

Built around your event.

  • 01

    Organizer Dashboard

    A private workspace to prepare and run your tournament, with registrations, schedules and results together.

  • 02

    Tournament Portal

    A public tournament page with event information, participants and results. Use it on its own or within your website.

  • 03

    Live Screen

    Live scores for venue screens, so players and spectators can follow the action.

  • 04

    TournStack Draw

    A prize draw for eligible players, spectators and entrants added by the organiser.

Lisbon.

Timeless Passion Padel Tournament

80+ playersAround 100 guests

An idea put into play.

The core system was used at the Timeless Passion Padel Tournament in Lisbon. Organizer tools, a public tournament page, live score displays and TournStack Draw came together for one event.

It was one early event. We’re now refining the system for pilots with sports centres and local clubs, learning what needs to work reliably across more events.

THE LONG VIEW

Room for every kind of tournament.

Sports centres and local clubs come first. Once TournStack has proved reliable across more events, we want to bring the same four-part experience to individual organisers too—from a backyard birthday tournament to a semi-pro competition.

That future personal offering would keep the Organizer Dashboard, Tournament Portal, Live Screen and TournStack Draw, adapted to the size of the event and carrying TournStack branding. We’ll add sports and formats as they’re tested, learning from real events along the way.
