A SIMPLE ACCESS MODEL

One organizer client.
Everyone else opens a link.

The planned organizer client is the only download. Players and spectators use browser links, while website widgets and venue displays bring event information to the places they already use.

TournStack is in development. The organizer client’s installation requirements will be confirmed during pilots.

WHO IT’S FOR

From your first tournament to a full season.

We want useful tournament tools to be within reach of independent organizers as well as sports centers.
