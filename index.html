Lisbon.

28 September 2026
Timeless Passion Padel Tournament

80+ players70+ spectators

An idea put into play.

The core system was used at the Timeless Passion Padel Tournament in Lisbon. Organizer tools, a public tournament page, live score displays and TournStack Draw came together for one event.

It was a real start, and one event. We’re now learning what needs to work reliably across more organizers and formats. We’ve begun conversations with a local league about testing TournStack.

THE LONG VIEW

Room for every kind of tournament.

We’re starting with organizers and sports centers. Once TournStack has proved reliable across more events, we want to bring the same four-part experience to everyday organizers too—from a backyard birthday tournament to a semi-pro competition.

That future personal offering would keep the Organizer Dashboard, Tournament Portal, Live Screen and TournStack Draw, adapted to the size of the event. We’ll add sports and formats as they’re tested, learning from real events along the way.
