Your tournament, connected.

We’re building tournament and league software with a simple goal: create your tournament in 10 minutes. The step-by-step Tournament Wizard creates player and guest registration forms, prepares match schedules and sets up the event emails you choose. During play, manage scores, changes and announcements from the Organizer Dashboard. Run multiple events across different sports.

Organisers use the dashboard on mobile or desktop. Players and guests register and follow online—no app download needed.

THE TOURNSTACK APPROACH

Built around your event.

  • 01

    Organizer Dashboard

    Create tournaments and manage leagues, registrations, schedules, scores and event changes from mobile or desktop.

  • 02

    Tournament Portal

    A public page where players and guests can register, find match times and follow results. Share it on its own or display it within your website.

  • 03

    Live Screen

    Show live scores and organiser announcements on venue screens, keeping players and spectators informed during the event.

  • 04

    TournStack Draw

    Run your event’s prize draw from the Organizer Dashboard, with eligible players, spectators and people you add.

HOW IT WORKS

From setup to the final score.

Less copying between forms, spreadsheets and email lists. A clearer view of registrations, matches and event changes.

01

Set it up

Follow the Tournament Wizard through the sport, format and event details. It explains the choices and prepares your registration forms and match schedule, with the event emails you choose.

02

Keep it moving

Check registrations and match times, enter scores and manage event changes from the Organizer Dashboard on mobile or desktop.

03

Keep everyone informed

Send one announcement to all of your event’s Tournament Portals and Live Screens.

Registration confirmations and event emails go out according to the settings you choose. You review the setup and make decisions during play.

YOUR EVENT, YOUR COMMUNITY

Keep your brand in front.

The B2B version can use your logo, colours and name, with the option to remove TournStack branding. Share your Tournament Portal on its own, within your website or on dedicated club pages. Players and guests follow through browser links.

TournStack is in development. Tell us about your event, and we’ll explain what a pilot could cover.

WHO IT’S FOR

For the people who make game day happen.

From a local tournament to a season of competition, the people running the event should have more room for the people taking part.

FOR SPORTS ORGANISERS

Let your community lead.

For individuals, agencies and organisations running tournaments or leagues—including sports centres and local clubs. If you run competitions regularly, we’re building around familiar workflows and connected event information, with the aim of making each new event less work to prepare.

Tell us about the events you organise.

FOR PERSONAL EVENTS

Bring the moving parts together.

Planning a birthday tournament or a competition with friends? That’s part of our longer-term direction too. We want guided setup and simple event control to work for first-time organisers, with broader personal access following the B2B experience.

Personal access is planned for a later stage.

THE LONG VIEW

From one tournament to a season of play.

We’re developing TournStack for one-off tournaments and ongoing leagues, from recreational events to regional and semi-pro competition. Sports organisers come first, with each sport and format tested as the product develops.

Broader personal access follows once the setup and event experience are ready. That version would retain TournStack branding, while players and spectators continue to use browser links to join and follow events.
