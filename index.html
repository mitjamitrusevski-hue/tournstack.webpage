THE APPROACH

Five ideas we’re building around.

A practical way to organize sport, from a single event to a regular competition calendar.

01

A multi-sport foundation

Padel and tennis come first. Our aim is to support more sports and competition formats as the platform develops.

02

Your website, connected

Embed registration, brackets and live schedules in your existing website. Keep your event close to your own brand and community.

03

One event or many

Accessible pricing is a core aim, whether you organize one tournament or several. Pay-per-event and subscription options are being explored.

04

One back office

The direction for sports centers: manage multiple tournaments, planned league formats and their results from one organizer workspace.

05

No player app needed

Players and spectators follow information through mobile-friendly pages and links. No download or separate app to install.
