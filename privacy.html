Email: info@tournstack.com

Contact us at that address about this notice or your personal information.

When you contact us

The contact form asks for your name, email address and inquiry. We also record the submission time. We use this information to answer you, discuss an event or possible pilot, and keep a record of the conversation. We do not add form submissions to a newsletter.

For ordinary inquiries, our legal basis is our legitimate interest in responding to people who contact us (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). If you ask us to discuss a potential agreement, processing needed to take those requested steps may also rely on Article 6(1)(b). The fields are needed so we can identify and answer your message. You can email us instead of using the form.

Cloudflare Turnstile checks whether a form submission is likely to be human. Cloudflare receives technical information from your browser for that check and sends a token, not your inquiry text, to our form endpoint. The form then sends the inquiry to Google Apps Script, which saves it in a Google Sheet after Cloudflare verifies the token. A Cloudflare Worker queues notification and confirmation email through mailbox.org. These services help us receive your message at info@tournstack.com and send a confirmation to the address you supplied.

Newsletter signup

The separate footer signup asks only for your email address and your agreement to receive TournStack news when our newsletter launches. It is optional and separate from contacting us. We use consent as the basis for this subscription (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)). You must confirm through a link sent to your email before your status becomes subscribed.

Cloudflare Turnstile checks the signup. Google Apps Script records your address, pending or confirmed status, request and confirmation times, the consent wording/version and signup source in a separate Newsletter tab in our private Contacts spreadsheet. Random confirmation and unsubscribe tokens are stored there only as hashes. The Cloudflare Worker queues the verification email through mailbox.org and processes your confirmation or unsubscribe action. No email address is included in these links. Opening an email link alone does not change your subscription: you confirm on the page.

You can cancel or unsubscribe using the link in your verification email, request a new unsubscribe link on our website, or email info@tournstack.com. Withdrawing consent does not affect earlier lawful processing. We are preparing the newsletter; signup does not promise a launch date or schedule.

Analytics and browser storage

We use Plausible Analytics to understand overall website traffic. Plausible says it does not place cookies, store raw IP addresses or create persistent visitor identifiers. We use its aggregate reports to learn which pages are useful. We count successful contact submissions, newsletter signup requests and newly confirmed newsletter subscriptions. We also measure email-link clicks, contact-link placement and which FAQ answers visitors open. Link clicks do not tell us whether an email was sent. Confirmation analytics use a fixed, token-free virtual URL (/newsletter-confirmed), rather than your private email link. For server-side confirmation counts, the visitor IP address and browser User-Agent are passed to Plausible for its normal aggregate processing; raw IP addresses are not stored by Plausible. These confirmation counts respect Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals. We do not send contact-form contents, newsletter addresses or confirmation tokens to Plausible.

CookieYes runs the consent banner and stores your choice in a necessary cookie named cookieyes-consent, currently set to expire after about one year. CookieYes may process your consent choice, a consent identifier, time and technical connection details to keep a record of your choice. You can change your choices through the banner’s revisit control. The optional invitation uses a timer held only in the current page’s memory. It does not read or write browser storage; navigating to another page starts a new timer. A successful inquiry also creates a one-use receipt marker, valid for ten minutes, so the thank-you page can count that saved submission once. It contains a random submission ID, expiry time and return URL, with no contact fields. The receipt is confined to your browser session. We do not share it with advertising providers.

When you make a new advertising choice, we also use browser local storage named tournstack-advertising-choice to link that choice to this notice version. It contains a consent identifier, notice version and expiry time, not contact-form details. It is valid for up to 180 days, is removed on withdrawal, and an expired record is removed the next time this website checks it. It does not authorise advertising on its own.

If you allow Meta, its _fbp cookie identifies your browser for advertising measurement. It is set on tournstack.com and has an observed expiry of about three months. See the Advertisement category in Cookie settings and Meta’s cookie information. Cookie expiry is separate from the retention of information already sent to a provider.

When permitted, LinkedIn’s optional service can use the following cookies on .linkedin.com. We gate that service under Advertisement even where a cookie supports its security or preferences. Browser settings and your LinkedIn session can affect which provider cookies are used. See LinkedIn’s cookie table.

  • bcookie: browser identification for diagnostics and abuse prevention; about one year.
  • li_gc: LinkedIn guest cookie preferences, separate from your TournStack choice; about 180 days.
  • lidc: LinkedIn data-centre selection; about one day.
  • __cf_bm: Cloudflare bot detection used by LinkedIn’s service; about 30 minutes.

Advertising tools and your choice

If you allow the Advertisement category in CookieYes, we use Meta Pixel and LinkedIn Insight Tag to measure visits and activity on our public Overview, Vision, FAQ and author pages and understand how our advertising performs. Our basis for this optional storage and related processing is your consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)). Refusing advertising does not prevent you from browsing, contacting us or managing a newsletter subscription.

Meta receives technical visit information, including the public page address and referrer, IP address, browser and device details, visit time and browser identifiers such as the _fbp cookie. Meta may connect this information with information it already holds, including account information. Its uses can include ad delivery and personalisation, personalised content, security, research and improving its products, as explained in Meta’s Privacy Policy.

Our Meta integration sends a page-view event after consent. Meta advanced matching and optional automatic-event collection are disabled. We do not send your name, form email address, inquiry text, newsletter records or private confirmation/unsubscribe links to Meta or LinkedIn. Contact, receipt, privacy and unsubscribe pages are excluded. We have not configured advertising conversions for contact or newsletter actions or created remarketing audiences for this integration.

You can reject advertising or turn Advertisement off using Cookie settings in the footer, then save your preferences. This stops new advertising requests and removes the advertising cookies our website can access. When LinkedIn has loaded, the page reloads to finish stopping its code. If you have unsent text, we warn you before opening preferences so you can finish or copy it first. A withdrawal from another tab stops new requests immediately and lets you copy unsent text before reloading. We do not save that text elsewhere. Withdrawal cannot recall information already sent, remove cookies on another provider’s domain or affect the lawfulness of earlier consent-based processing. The providers’ own privacy and advertising controls offer additional choices.

LinkedIn receives technical visit information such as the public page address, referrer, IP address, browser/device details and time. Its Website Actions feature automatically records visits and button/form activity, including public page titles and button labels; it is not limited to page views. We do not configure enhanced matching or supply form-field values to LinkedIn. It may connect visit information with information it already holds, including account information, to provide advertising measurement, audience insights and ad-related services and improve its products. See LinkedIn’s Privacy Policy and Website Actions information. We have not created remarketing audiences or approved automatic conversion suggestions for this integration.

Newsletter subscription, advertising consent and our separate cookie-free analytics are different choices and purposes. Choices made under the earlier Meta-only notice do not enable the expanded advertising setup: we ask for a new choice.

Who helps us run the website

We use GitHub Pages to host the site and Cloudflare to deliver and protect website traffic, CookieYes to manage cookie choices, Plausible for aggregate analytics, Google Apps Script and Sheets to receive inquiries, Cloudflare Turnstile to check form submissions, Cloudflare Workers to queue contact and newsletter-verification emails and process newsletter confirmation links, and mailbox.org to send and receive them. These providers may process information needed to deliver their services, such as connection details or the inquiry you submit. We do not sell your contact details.

GitHub, CookieYes, Google and Cloudflare are providers that may process data across borders, including outside the European Economic Area. The safeguards for a particular transfer depend on the provider and service, and may include an adequacy decision or standard contractual clauses. For details about safeguards relevant to your inquiry, contact us at info@tournstack.com.

For Meta Pixel, the EU provider is Meta Platforms Ireland Limited. Under the Meta Business Tools Terms and Controller Addendum, we and Meta Ireland are joint controllers for the specified collection of visit information and its transmission to Meta for advertising and personalisation purposes. We are responsible for explaining that collection and implementing the tool correctly. The arrangement assigns Meta responsibility for enabling access, correction, deletion and portability rights concerning information stored by Meta after transmission. You may exercise your rights against either party; we forward relevant requests to Meta and cooperate with it.

Meta independently controls its subsequent processing outside that joint operation. Its role differs for measurement and analytics services it provides on our instructions, where its terms identify it as a processor. Meta’s Privacy Policy explains its own legal bases, contact details and rights procedures.

Meta may transfer, store and process information outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Meta’s policy states that it relies on adequacy decisions where applicable, including the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework for covered transfers to certified Meta Platforms, Inc. It states that other transfers may rely on European Commission-approved standard contractual clauses or applicable legal derogations. These mechanisms depend on the destination and service; they are not a guarantee that all information stays in Europe. See the international-transfer section of Meta’s Privacy Policy for safeguards and how to request further information.

For people in the EU/EEA and Switzerland, LinkedIn’s Privacy Policy identifies LinkedIn Ireland Unlimited Company as its controller. Its Independent Controller Addendum covers the relevant Insight Tag processing, subject to the exclusions in those terms. LinkedIn controls its own uses of that information; it is not simply our processor for all advertising activity.

LinkedIn processes information internationally, including in the United States. Its terms provide controller-to-controller standard contractual clauses for qualifying EU transfers. Its transfer information also describes adequacy decisions and Data Privacy Framework participation where applicable. The mechanism depends on the recipient and transfer. See LinkedIn’s transfer safeguards and its Privacy Policy for further information and rights contacts.

How long we keep information

Unconfirmed newsletter requests are removed after seven days. Confirmed subscriptions are retained for up to 24 months from confirmation. After unsubscribe, we keep a limited inactive record and status-change time for up to 24 months from the change, to honour your choice and retain the request history. Inactive addresses are excluded from newsletter sends. A new signup requires fresh email confirmation before reactivation. You can also contact us to request deletion.

We keep contact-form inquiries and related email conversations for up to 24 months after our last exchange with you, unless a legal obligation or an active dispute requires longer. We review the Google Sheet and mailbox records for deletion under this period.

The Cloudflare email queue is configured to remove job data and deduplication records after 30 days; successful email payloads are cleared after SMTP acceptance. Inquiry-related copies in the mailbox and inquiry entries in Google Sheets follow the inquiry retention period above. Newsletter records follow their separate periods stated above.

Meta’s Business Tools Terms permit retention of Event Data for a maximum of two years. This is separate from the approximately three-month _fbp cookie and the 180-day validity of our advertising-choice marker.

LinkedIn states that direct identifiers associated with Insight Tag visit data are removed within seven days and that the remaining pseudonymous data is deleted within 180 days. These are provider retention periods, separate from cookies and our consent marker. See the Insight Tag FAQ.

CookieYes records consent choices. Its published Free-plan retention information provides one year of consent-log export availability and one month of dashboard search/view access. These service periods are separate from the approximately one-year cookieyes-consent browser cookie. See CookieYes consent-log information.

Your choices and rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, restrict its use, object to processing based on legitimate interests, or receive a portable copy where that right applies. Write to info@tournstack.com; we may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. You may also complain to a data protection authority, particularly where you live or work or where you believe an infringement occurred. In Slovenia, you can contact the Information Commissioner.

The automated check described above screens submissions for spam. If it prevents you from using the form, you can contact us by email. When this notice or the website’s data practices change, we will update this page and its date.
