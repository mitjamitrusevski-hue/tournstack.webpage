About the author

Mitja Mitruševski.

Vision & Commercial Lead · TournStack website author

His work at TournStack

Mitja leads TournStack’s vision and commercial work and writes the first-party content on this website. His subjects include guided tournament setup, automated registration and event emails, simple organiser controls, public event information and league management. He explains the product’s development priorities and how its parts fit together for organisers, players and spectators.

The project's core system was used at the Timeless Passion Padel Tournament in Lisbon on 28 September 2026. That single event is a starting point for learning; it is not a claim that TournStack has been validated across multiple organisers. His writing reflects the current plan to refine the product with sports organisers before broader personal use.

Professional background

Outside TournStack, Mitja is Head of Marketing at Timeless Tech. His published biography describes earlier graphic design and art direction work in casino technology at Gold Club and Win Systems. That product and communications background informs how he presents TournStack's work.
