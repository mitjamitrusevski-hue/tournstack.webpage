About the author

Mitja Mitruševski.

Vision & Commercial Lead · TournStack website author

Mitja writes the first-party content on this website and leads TournStack's vision and commercial work. The project grew from a familiar place: time spent around sport and the work it takes to keep a tournament moving.

The core system was used at the Timeless Passion Padel Tournament in Lisbon on 28 September 2026. That event gave the team a real starting point for learning what organizers and players need. TournStack is still in development, and the team is in conversation with a local league about further testing.

What he writes here

Mitja currently authors the project overview, vision, answers to common questions and other first-party website copy. TournStack publishes the site. Technical and product claims are checked against current project evidence before publication.

His Timeless Tech biography describes earlier work in another industry; it does not serve as a sports credential.
