About the author

Mitja Mitruševski.

Vision & Commercial Lead · TournStack website author

His work at TournStack

Mitja leads TournStack's vision and commercial work and writes the first-party content on this website. He explains what the team is building, whom it is for and which parts are still in development. His subjects include tournament organisation, public event information, club branding and the experience of players and spectators following an event.

The project's core system was used at the Timeless Passion Padel Tournament in Lisbon on 28 September 2026. That single event is a starting point for learning; it is not a claim that TournStack has been validated across multiple organisers. Mitja's writing reflects the current plan to refine the product with sports centres and local clubs before broader personal use.

Professional background

Outside TournStack, Mitja is Head of Marketing at Timeless Tech. His published biography describes earlier graphic design and art direction work in casino technology at Gold Club and Win Systems. That product and communications background informs how he presents TournStack's work. It is not a sports-operations, engineering or legal qualification.
