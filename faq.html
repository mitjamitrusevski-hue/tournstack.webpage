Getting started

What TournStack is, where it stands and who it is for.

01What is TournStack?

TournStack is a tournament-management project in development. It brings organizer work and public tournament information together, starting with padel and tennis.

02Who is TournStack for?

We are starting with people who organize events: independent organizers, clubs and sports centers. Players and spectators use the public experience to follow an event; they are not the same as the person buying organizer tools.

03Can I use TournStack now?

TournStack is in development. Its core system has been used at one live tournament in Lisbon. We are discussing further testing with a local league and other organizers. Tell us about your event if you would like to discuss the current scope.

04Which sports are you starting with?

Padel and tennis are the initial focus. We want to support more sports and formats over time, after the first workflows have been tested. A future sport is not a feature available today.

05Will there be a version for people organizing their own tournaments?

That is the longer-term aim. We want the same four core parts (Organizer Dashboard, Tournament Portal, Live Screen and TournStack Draw) to serve events from a backyard birthday tournament to semi-pro competition, with much of the larger offering adapted to smaller events. We will work toward wider access only after repeated events show the experience is reliable.

Running an event

The organizer workspace and the public tournament page.

06What is the Tournament Portal?

The Tournament Portal is a complete public page for a tournament. People can use it to follow the event. It can stand alone or appear within an existing website.

07Do I need my own website?

No. The Tournament Portal can stand alone. If you already have a website, it can also appear there.

08Can the same Tournament Portal appear on several pages?

Yes. The same portal can appear on several pages at the same time. We will confirm the supported setup and limits for a particular pilot rather than promise every website configuration.

09What is the difference between the Portal and a widget?

The Tournament Portal is the complete public tournament page. A widget is an individual component added to another page. Embedding describes where something appears; it does not mean the whole portal is only a small widget.

10What does the Organizer Dashboard do?

It is the private workspace for people running the tournament. The core system brings tournament setup, participants, courts, schedules and results together. We are still testing the exact external-organizer workflow.

11Will organizers need to download an app?

The intended setup includes a downloadable organizer client. The installation requirements and release platforms are still being confirmed. This is separate from the public pages that players and spectators open in a browser.

12Do players or spectators need to download an app?

The current direction gives players and spectators browser links to follow the tournament. A separate native player app is not part of the current roadmap.

13Can I run several tournaments or a league?

A connected workspace for repeated events is part of the direction for clubs and sports centers. League support is planned. We will describe exact formats and availability when they are tested and confirmed.

Following the event

Scores, the community draw and business branding.

14Can players register and follow results online?

Online registration and public tournament information are part of the product direction. Public tournament components were used in the Lisbon event. We are refining the player-facing workflow through further testing.

15What does Live Screen show?

Live Screen shows scores while an event is live. It is a separate display for people following the action at the venue.

16Does Live Screen show video or pre-event information?

No. It is a live score display, not a match broadcast. Pre-event rosters, venue details, weather and contact numbers are not part of its confirmed scope.

17What is TournStack Draw?

TournStack Draw is a separate prize-draw experience for an event. A digital draw was used at the Lisbon tournament. The exact controls for future organizers are still being confirmed.

18Who can take part in TournStack Draw?

The participant pool can include eligible players, spectators and people added manually. Each event still needs its own clear eligibility rules.

19Can the draw use ticket entries?

Ticket support is not currently available. Ask us about the participant options confirmed for the event you have in mind.

20Can a club use its own logo, colours and name?

Yes, those branding options are part of the B2B version. We will confirm the scope available for your event. A future personal version would keep TournStack branding.

21Can TournStack branding be removed?

The B2B version includes an option to remove TournStack branding. That option does not apply to the planned personal offering.

Proof, plans and contact

What we have learned and what to discuss next.

22Has TournStack been used at a real tournament?

Yes. The core system was used at the Timeless Passion Padel Tournament in Lisbon on 28 September 2026, with organizer tools, a public tournament page, live score displays and a digital draw. It was one event, not proof of repeated external use. See the Lisbon event.

23Are you testing TournStack with other organizers?

We have begun conversations with a local league about testing the app. A meeting is not a completed pilot; we will share what we learn after further events actually take place.

24When will TournStack be on Google Play or the App Store?

There is no confirmed store listing or release date. Wider personal use is a longer-term goal, and we want the core experience to prove reliable through repeated events first.

25How much will TournStack cost?

Pricing has not been confirmed. We are exploring options for one-off and recurring organizers and will discuss any current pilot terms directly, without presenting an untested price as settled.

26Will players have to pay TournStack to follow an event?

The intended commercial model charges organizers for the software, not players who follow an event. An organizer may separately set an entry fee for its tournament. Final terms are still being tested.

27Why build this when organizers already use spreadsheets and group chats?

Those tools help people get an event started, but a court change or late result can require the same update in several places. We are building TournStack to connect organizer work with the public tournament page. We have not yet measured a general time-saving claim.

28How do I know whether it fits my event?

Tell us about your sport, format, venue and what is hardest to coordinate. We can explain the current scope, show the parts already demonstrated and discuss whether a pilot would be useful.

29How can I talk to the team or suggest a partnership?

Use the contact form to tell us about your event, a possible pilot or an idea worth exploring together. You can also email info@tournstack.com.
