Getting started

Guided setup, current development and who it is for.

01What is TournStack?

TournStack is a tournament-and-league management project in development. We’re building guided tournament setup with a ten-minute goal, automated registration forms and event emails, and simple control during play. Public tournament pages, live score displays and a prize draw keep organisers, players and spectators connected.

02Who is TournStack for?

We’re starting with sports centres and local clubs. Independent organisers are part of the longer-term direction. Players and spectators can follow public event information in a browser.

03Can I use TournStack now?

TournStack is in development. Its core system has been used at one live tournament in Lisbon. We’re refining it for pilots with sports centres and local clubs. Tell us about your event if you would like to discuss the current scope.

04How does the Tournament Wizard work?

The Tournament Wizard guides you through creating a tournament from scratch, with a ten-minute initial setup goal. It explains supported sport and format choices, so prior sport-format or tournament-organising knowledge is not expected. You supply the event details and review the settings; TournStack creates player and guest registration forms and handles configured event emails. The ten-minute goal is still being tested.

05Which sports are you starting with?

Our early live reference is a padel tournament. The wider development scope includes tennis, football, basketball, volleyball, beach volleyball and climbing, with sport-specific formats tested before release. We will confirm the supported sport and format for each pilot or release.

06Will there be a version for people organising their own tournaments?

Yes, broader personal access is the longer-term aim. We want guided setup and connected event tools to serve occasions from a backyard birthday tournament to semi-pro competition. B2B clubs and sports centres come first; the personal offering follows once the setup and event experience are ready and would retain TournStack branding.

Running an event

The dashboard, event decisions and announcements.

07What is the Tournament Portal?

The Tournament Portal is a complete public page for a tournament. People can use it to follow the event. It can stand alone or appear within an existing website.

08Do I need my own website?

No. The Tournament Portal can stand alone. If you already have a website, it can also appear there.

09Can the same Tournament Portal appear on several pages?

Yes. The same portal can appear on several pages at the same time. We will confirm the supported setup and limits for a particular pilot rather than promise every website configuration.

10What is the difference between the Portal and a widget?

The Tournament Portal is the complete public tournament page. A widget is an individual component added to another page. Embedding describes where something appears; it does not mean the whole portal is only a small widget.

11What does the Organizer Dashboard do?

It is the private workspace for running your event. Record scores, manage changes such as injuries, withdrawals or substitutions, and send announcements to your event’s public pages and live score displays. TournStack connects the information; organisers make the decisions and enter the relevant updates.

12Can I send an announcement during an event?

Yes. The development scope lets organisers send one announcement from the Organizer Dashboard to all of their event’s connected Tournament Portals and Live Screens. Here, “broadcast” means an event message. It does not mean live video or audio.

13Will organizers need to download an app?

The intended setup includes a downloadable organizer client. The installation requirements and release platforms are still being confirmed. This is separate from the public pages that players and spectators open in a browser.

14Do players or spectators need to download an app?

No. Players can register and follow through browser links, while spectators can open the public tournament page or view the venue’s live score displays. Any downloadable organiser client is separate from this public experience. TournStack is still in development; current pilot access is confirmed with the team.

15Can I run several tournaments or a league?

Tournaments and leagues are both part of TournStack’s development scope. We’re building a connected workspace for one-off events and ongoing club competitions. Supported league formats and availability will be confirmed for each release or pilot. The ten-minute setup goal refers to creating a tournament, not configuring an entire season.

Following the event

Scores, public event views, the draw and branding.

16Can players register and follow results online?

Online registration and public tournament information are part of the product direction. Public tournament components were used in the Lisbon event. We are refining the player-facing workflow through further testing.

17What does Live Screen show?

Live Screen shows scores and organiser announcements while the event is live. It gives people at the venue a shared view of the action and relevant event updates.

18Does Live Screen show video or pre-event information?

No. Live Screen displays scores and event announcements. It does not show match video or pre-event participant lists, venue details, weather or contact numbers.

19What is TournStack Draw?

TournStack Draw is a separate prize-draw experience for an event. A digital draw was used at the Lisbon tournament. The exact controls for future organizers are still being confirmed.

20Who can take part in TournStack Draw?

The participant pool can include eligible players, spectators and people added manually. Each event still needs its own clear eligibility rules.

21Can the draw use ticket entries?

Ticket support is not currently available. Ask us about the participant options confirmed for the event you have in mind.

22Can a club use its own logo, colours and name?

Yes, those branding options are part of the B2B version. We will confirm the scope available for your event. A future personal version would keep TournStack branding.

23Can TournStack branding be removed?

The B2B version includes an option to remove TournStack branding. That option does not apply to the planned personal offering.

Proof, plans and contact

What we have learned and what to discuss next.

24Has TournStack been used at a real tournament?

Yes. The core system was used at the Timeless Passion Padel Tournament in Lisbon on 28 September 2026, with organizer tools, a public tournament page, live score displays and a digital draw. It was one event, not proof of repeated external use. See the Lisbon event.

25Are you testing TournStack with other organizers?

We’re refining TournStack for pilots with sports centres and local clubs. We’ll share what we learn after further events actually take place.

26When will TournStack be on Google Play or the App Store?

There is no confirmed store listing or release date. Wider personal use is a longer-term goal, and we want the core experience to prove reliable through repeated events first.

27How much will TournStack cost?

Pricing has not been confirmed. We are exploring options for one-off and recurring organizers and will discuss any current pilot terms directly, without presenting an untested price as settled.

28Will players have to pay TournStack to follow an event?

The intended commercial model charges organizers for the software, not players who follow an event. An organizer may separately set an entry fee for its tournament. Final terms are still being tested.

29Why build this when organisers already use spreadsheets and group chats?

A court change or late result can mean updating several places and chasing people for information. We’re building TournStack to guide initial setup, create registration resources and handle configured event emails, then connect organiser updates with the public event views. Organisers still control what happens during play.

30How do I know whether it fits my event?

Tell us about your sport, format, venue and what is hardest to coordinate. We can explain the current scope, show the parts already demonstrated and discuss whether a pilot would be useful.

31How can I talk to the team or suggest a partnership?

Use the contact form to tell us about your event, a possible pilot or an idea worth exploring together. You can also email info@tournstack.com.
