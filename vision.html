Overview/Our vision

Our vision · In development

Keep organizing simple.
Keep sport at the center.

The people who make an event happen should have room to enjoy it too.

WHY WE’RE BUILDING IT

The work behind the game matters.

A late arrival, a changed court or a score buried in a group chat can pull an organizer away from the moment. We’re building TournStack to bring that work together and make the public tournament easier to follow.

We care about the play, the people between matches and the community that turns an event into more than a bracket.

THE APPROACH

Your event stays yours.

The Organizer Dashboard gives the people running an event a private workspace. The public Tournament Portal can stand alone or appear on an existing website. Live Screen shows scores during play, and TournStack Draw adds a separate community moment.

For clubs and sports centers, the B2B version can use their logo, colours and name, with the option to remove TournStack branding. The product supports the event; the organizer’s community stays in front.

WHERE WE ARE

One live start. More to learn.

The core system was used at the Timeless Passion Padel Tournament in Lisbon on 28 September 2026. That was one live deployment, and we’re now working toward repeated testing with other organizers.

We’ve already begun conversations with a local league about testing TournStack. We want to learn where the experience helps, where it needs work and how reliably it holds up through real events.

See the Lisbon event ↗

THE LONG VIEW

From club tournaments to anyone’s game day.

Padel and tennis are our starting point. In time, we want TournStack to support more sports and formats, and to be useful from a backyard birthday tournament through to semi-pro competition.

A future personal offering would keep the same four core parts—Organizer Dashboard, Tournament Portal, Live Screen and TournStack Draw—with much of the larger offering’s capability adapted to smaller events. It would carry TournStack branding. We want to see it work reliably across repeated events before inviting a wider audience in.
