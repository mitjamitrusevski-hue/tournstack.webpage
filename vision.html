Overview/Our vision

Our vision · In development

Keep organizing simple.
Keep sport at the center.

The people who make an event happen should have room to enjoy it too.

WHY WE’RE BUILDING IT

The work behind the game matters.

A late arrival, a changed court or a score buried in a group chat can pull an organizer away from the moment. We’re building TournStack to bring that work together and make the public tournament easier to follow.

We care about the play, the people between matches and the community that turns an event into more than a bracket.

THE APPROACH

Your event stays yours.

The Organizer Dashboard is a private workspace for registrations, schedules and results. The Tournament Portal is a complete public event page that can stand alone or appear within an existing website. Live Screen shows live scores at the venue, and TournStack Draw brings eligible players, spectators and manually added entrants into a prize draw.

For clubs and sports centers, the B2B version can use their logo, colours and name, with the option to remove TournStack branding. The product supports the event; the organizer’s community stays in front.

WHERE WE ARE

One live start. More to learn.

The core system was used at the Timeless Passion Padel Tournament in Lisbon on 28 September 2026. We’re now refining it for pilots with sports centres and local clubs, focusing on reliable event workflows and feedback from the people using it.

It was one early event. Further pilots will help us learn where the experience helps and where it needs work.

See the Lisbon event ↗

THE LONG VIEW

From club tournaments to anyone’s game day.

Padel and tennis are our starting point. In time, we want TournStack to support more sports and formats, and to be useful from a backyard birthday tournament through to semi-pro competition.

B2B comes first. Once we’re comfortable with the product’s reliability and setup experience, we plan to bring TournStack to individual organisers through Google Play, the Apple App Store and similar channels. That future offering would carry TournStack branding, with players and spectators still able to follow public tournament information in their browser.
