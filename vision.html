Overview/Our vision

Our vision · In development

Keep organizing simple.
Keep sport at the center.

The people who make an event happen should have room to enjoy it too.

WHY WE’RE BUILDING IT

The work behind the game matters.

We’re sports enthusiasts, and we know how much work happens before the first match. Organising our own events meant checking registrations, updating lists, sending reminders and passing on the same information in several places.

That’s why we started TournStack. We want guided setup and automated routine tasks to leave organisers more time to welcome people, keep matches moving and deal with the things that need their attention.

THE APPROACH

Your event stays yours.

Create the tournament step by step, then use the Organizer Dashboard to review registrations, manage schedules, enter scores and send announcements. Players and guests follow the Tournament Portal, while Live Screen shows scores and event messages at the venue. TournStack Draw is the prize-draw module inside the dashboard. We’re building this around both tournaments and leagues.

For sports organisers, the B2B version can use your logo, colours and name, with the option to remove TournStack branding. The product supports the event; your community stays in front.

WHERE WE ARE

One live start. More to learn.

The core system was used at the Timeless Passion Padel Tournament in Lisbon on 28 September 2026. We’re now refining it for pilots with sports organisers, focusing on reliable event workflows and feedback from the people using it.

We’ll use the next pilots to improve setup, event control and support, based on what organisers tell us.

See the Lisbon event ↗

THE LONG VIEW

From a tournament to a season of play.

Our early live reference comes from padel. We’re developing tournament and league tools across tennis, football, basketball, volleyball, beach volleyball and climbing, with each sport and format tested before release.

Sports organisers come first: individuals, agencies and organisations running tournaments or leagues, including sports centres and local clubs. Once the core setup and event experience are ready, we plan broader personal access through Google Play, the Apple App Store and similar channels. That future offering would carry TournStack branding. Players and guests would still join and follow through browser links.
